2026-10-25 with the same driver; nvidia-smi's figure is the whole card's, so other programs move
it, and the margin's 1,611 MB floor covers it either way.

  printing the WDDM driver's "not available", not a measure. The bench reads the request, the free
  memory and the cap correctly from the same message.
