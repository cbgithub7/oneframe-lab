no-spill claim were never tested on the card.

## Findings

1. **The bench learns within its own run.** `make_scheduler` passes `LearnedStore(None)`, and
   its docstring says that means "nothing learned (estimates stay uncorrected)". In fact
   `LearnedStore(None)` keeps what it learns in memory, and every later fit in the same bench
   reads it.
    - The control spill (fit off, 7,822 MB measured against an estimate of 2,764 MB) recorded a
      working-memory ratio of about 3.875 on `cuda:6.1`.
    - `_correction` takes the largest recent ratio, so the overrun's card estimate grew about 3.9
      times and no longer fit the 2,825 MB budget the bench had set from the uncorrected
      estimate.
    - The fit then took the cpu at full quality, as decision 7 says.

   The numbers bear this out. With working memory scaled 3.875 times, the card fits only with
   changes 0, 1 and 2 (about 2,279 MB), and that is the faster alternative the overrun's
   `node.fit` offered.
2. **The holder is sized without its own CUDA context.** The holder is a separate torch process,
   so it creates a context of its own (94 MB here) on top of what it holds.
   `hold = free - (need + 32 + margin)` leaves that out. The budget came out 2,309 MB instead of
   about 2,409, under the 2,377 MB the node needs after change 0. The fit rightly fell to the cpu.
3. **The report reads "done" for overruns that never overran.** Both overrun rows show `done`
   with `step_oom` 0 and `oom` 0, on the cpu. Nothing says that the step's precondition (a card
   fit at the estimate) was not met, though the plan asks the report to say plainly what was not
   measured.

## Also seen

- On the cpu, the measured peak is about 493 MB above what the node allocates: the torch
  runtime's own footprint. The holder run learned it as a cpu working-memory ratio of 1.28.
- The control spill rose only 67 MB for `spill_mb` 256: the child saw 7,565 MB free and
  allocated 7,822 MB. The counter does rise, but a larger spill would make the control clearer.
- Nothing persisted on this machine beyond the run logs: no `memory/learned.json` was written
  under the data root.
- The session could not watch the screen during the deliberate spill.
