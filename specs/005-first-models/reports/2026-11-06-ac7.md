## Findings

1. **MoGe's peak is the larger of two parts, which a sum of terms cannot say.** From 1536 px to the
   full photo, level 0's peak rose 648 MB (MoGe-3) and level 9's 27: the network's part grows with
   the level, the part at the photo's size with its pixels, and the peak is whichever is larger.
   A sum of terms covering both over-estimates at 7.2 MP: at levels 5 and 9 the peaks are 79 to
   80 % of their estimates (2,888 MB for 3,669). That wastes memory, never a run; a `max` in the
   formula, or another form, is the cloud's to weigh, with a larger photo to fit on.
2. **The system figures pass by less than 1 MB, MoGe-2's level 0 by 0.0.** They do not depend on
   the photo, so the full photo only repeats the fit's runs, which fail only by noise (the spec's
   Decisions). The margin is the engine's (spec 002), not the figure's.
3. **bench:fit's report names collide and name no card.** Every report of one node on one day is
   `<date>-<node>-fit.md` (`report_path` looks for a `card` the record never has), so a second run
   overwrites the first; the scripts here moved each one aside. The report's heading gives no card
   or machine, so a report alone cannot say where it ran (this one does).
4. **Depth Pro's fp32 figure on a card is low by the reserve.** It is the spike's allocated figure;
   by fp16's, torch's reserve on a card is about 18 % more. Kept as the spec says, until a card
   that holds fp32 reports; the processor's fp32 peak is under it (0.968).
5. **AC1's test needed a 10 GB card** (`0ad0551`, the owner's go-ahead). With these figures Depth
   Pro needs about 6.2 GB at fp16 and 9.2 GB at fp32; no test machine had a card budget between
   them, so the test reached neither fp16 branch. A 10 GB card of compute 8.6 on a 16 GB machine
   reaches both. Nothing in the test changed.
6. **MoGe-3's process holds its refiner's tensors:** 154 MB more system memory than MoGe-2's,
   about the difference in their checkpoints' sizes (157 MB), though the node does not load them
   into the network.
